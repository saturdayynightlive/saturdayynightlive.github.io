Simply5x5

An ad-free iOS workout tracker for 5x5 strength training. Solo project, released on the App Store.

SwiftUI · Firebase AI Logic · HealthKit · Swift Charts

App Store CV (PDF)

Implementation

  • Workout logging with automatic weight progression and deloads.
  • A plate calculator and estimated one-rep max tracking.
  • Training feedback using Gemini through Firebase AI Logic.
  • HealthKit integration and progress visualization with Swift Charts.
